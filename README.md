# About the Movie: CZ12 (Chinese Zodiac)

## 🎬 Movie Overview
**Title:** CZ12 *(Chinese Zodiac)*  
**Release Year:** 2012  
**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Comedy  
**Director & Lead Actor:** Jackie Chan  
**Also Known As:** *Armour of God 3: Chinese Zodiac*

---

## 📖 Plot Summary
Jackie Chan plays **JC**, a treasure hunter and adventurer tasked with recovering the stolen bronze heads of the **12 Chinese Zodiac animals**, artifacts originally looted during the 19th century.  

The mission takes him through multiple countries and dangerous situations. Using his martial arts skills, creative stunts, and fast thinking, JC fights criminals, protects cultural heritage, and shows loyalty to history and homeland.
